## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is rapidly changing how software is designed, written, and delivered. But as AI-generated code becomes a larger part of the development lifecycle, critical questions emerge: How do we ensure quality? Who is responsible when things go wrong? And what role do engineers play when the code they're reviewing wasn't written by a human?
 
Join Fifth Third Bank technology leaders Justin Behymer, Eric Rolf, and Chris Van Dyke for a panel discussion facilitated by Christine Eichhold, exploring the realities of software development in an AI-assisted world. Drawing on their firsthand experiences, the panelists will examine how AI is reshaping code reviews, engineering accountability, and software quality practices. Attendees will gain insights into the risks and opportunities of AI-generated code, the evolving responsibilities of developers and reviewers, and the impact these technologies are having on engineering teams and organizational trust.
 
Whether you're a developer, architect, engineering leader, or technology enthusiast, this session will challenge assumptions and spark conversation around one of the most important questions facing modern software teams: when the code is written by a machine, how do we maintain confidence in the systems we build?
