## Abstract

Many IT professionals want to advance—but get stuck waiting for the “right opportunity,” promotion, or title. The reality is that in some cases career progression doesn’t start with a role change—it starts with a shift in how you think about your work, your time, and your impact.

Drawing on concepts from The Leadership Pipeline this session provides a practical roadmap for moving from individual contributor to higher-level roles by building influence, visibility, and leadership credibility before formal authority exists. 

Attendees will learn about a practical roadmap for building leadership capability before the title comes—so when the opportunity appears, you’re already operating at the next level.
