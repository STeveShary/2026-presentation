## Abstract

Great engineers don’t just build, they connect and keep connecting.

The fastest way to better ideas, sharper execution, and more interesting work isn’t isolation, it’s exposure. Show up. Be curious. Talk about everything you are seeing, even when it is messy. Every conversation compounds: new tools, better patterns, unexpected collaborators. The difference is proximity to the right people and problems they are solving.

Don’t build alone.
