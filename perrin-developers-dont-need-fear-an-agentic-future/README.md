## Abstract

The talk in the news is all doom and gloom with a healthy dose of anxiety thrown in for good measure.  Layoffs, AI's impending self-awareness, job loss from AI.

The future of technology employment is certainly unclear now and it's going to be tough for a while, but Jevons Paradox, the calculator, and the computer itself proves that the future is bright for developers.
