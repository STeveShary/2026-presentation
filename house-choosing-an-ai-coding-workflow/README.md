## Abstract

AI is here, but deciding what to use and how is difficult. In this fast-paced session, we'll explore key decisions for coding effectively in AI including IDEs, models, harnesses, instructions, permissions, agents, and more. You'll leave this session with a variety of ideas for how to rethink your AI-related tooling, patterns, and workflows.
