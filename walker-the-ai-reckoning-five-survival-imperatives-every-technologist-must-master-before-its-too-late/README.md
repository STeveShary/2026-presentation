## Abstract

The tidal wave of AI technologies is undeniable and quickly becoming the fabric of modern business. In this provocative keynote, Mike J. Walker challenges technologists to face the great AI reckoning, a fast redistribution of competitive advantage where those who rethink how products are built, how employees are empowered, and how decisions are made, will define the next decade. Those who wait, will fall victim to the AI Reckoning.

Attendees will leave with a clear understanding of the five survival imperatives that will reshape every technology organization along with understanding how to shift from AI from experimentation to AI native organization. Mike will provide his visionary but actionable insights on the bold questions that challenges long-held assumptions about architecture, development, data, and leadership.
