# AI Can Make Mistakes: Stories from a Test Engineer

Anna Chernova &mdash; *SDET at Ingage Partners*

## Bio

Anna Chernova is a Senior SDET with more than 10 years of experience in software testing, automation, and quality engineering who enjoys breaking things before customers do.

Her recent fascination with AI started as a productivity experiment and quickly turned into a collection of lessons learned, surprising successes, and memorable failures. Whether generating API tests, reviewing requirements, or attempting to improve a cookie recipe, Anna has discovered that AI is often helpful, occasionally brilliant, and sometimes confidently wrong.

She is passionate about sharing practical, real-world experiences that help engineers use new technologies more effectively while keeping a healthy dose of curiosity and critical thinking.

## Abstract

Everyone talks about how AI can make developers and testers more productive. Fewer people talk about what happens when AI is wrong.

Over the last year, I started using AI for everything from generating API tests and automation code to debugging failures, reviewing requirements, and even helping me bake cookies. Sometimes it saved hours of work. Sometimes it confidently suggested APIs that didn't exist, generated tests that looked perfect but missed critical defects, misunderstood requirements, or recommended recipe changes that turned out to be a terrible idea.

In this session, I'll share real examples of where AI helped, where it failed, and what those failures taught me about using AI responsibly in software engineering.
